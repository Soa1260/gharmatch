# Incident response and admin moderation

A report is a reason to look, not a finding. Protect the person first, then decide fairly. Review at `/admin/reports`. The reporter is emailed at each step, without details.

## Severity and the first response
| Severity | Categories | What the system does | Your target |
|---|---|---|---|
| Critical | I need help now, physical threat or stalking, someone entered without permission | Listing or search paused at once | Look within 1 hour in the daytime |
| High | Landlord harassment, hidden cameras, unsafe property, suspicious representative, financial fraud | Listing or search paused at once | Same day |
| Medium | Occupancy not as disclosed, discriminatory rules, other | Paused when two different people report | Within 2 days |

Pausing is a precaution. The owner sees that a report exists, and can appeal.

## Steps
1. **Start review.** Marks it Under review and tells the reporter someone is looking.
2. **Read the saved copy.** It is the item as it was when reported, with the messages between the reporter and that listing. It does not change if the owner edits.
3. **Decide.** Take action, or dismiss. Write a reason (3 to 500 characters). It goes into the audit log and the reporter is emailed the outcome.
   - *Take action* pauses and holds the listing or search, or bans the account.
   - *Dismiss* closes the report. A hold already placed is not lifted automatically. Use "Release hold" if the reason for holding is gone.
4. **Appeals.** The owner can explain their side from their dashboard. Uphold (lifts the hold and closes the report as dismissed) or reject, with a reason. They are emailed.

## When to involve the police
- Any threat to life or physical safety, stalking, sexual harassment, assault, unlawful entry, or a significant financial fraud: encourage the reporter to call 112 and to file a complaint, and tell them the 181 women helpline can help.
- Preserve the saved copy. Give records to police only on a valid legal request from the proper authority, through the person responsible for legal requests, after checking the request. Do not hand out anything informally.
- If you believe someone is in immediate danger and you can identify them, say so to 112 and keep a record of what you shared and why.

## Account suspension
Suspend (ban) when: credible threats or stalking, repeated harassment, a confirmed fake or fraudulent listing, or hidden surveillance. A suspension is visible to the user and can be appealed. A landlord with an upheld safety report can no longer invite women or mixed groups.

## False and coordinated reports
- Do not assume an accusation is true. Look for the same few accounts reporting many listings, brand-new accounts reporting a rival, copied wording.
- The system already stops automatic holds for people with 3 or more dismissed reports in 30 days. Their reports still reach you.
- If you find abuse of the reporting system, suspend the reporting accounts too.

## Record keeping
- Every admin action is in the audit log (who, what, when, why).
- Reports and saved copies are kept while they may be needed. Decide and publish a retention period with legal advice.
- Sensitive details in reports are for the admins who need them only. Do not copy them into chat tools or email.

## Review
Look at all closed reports monthly. Update the word lists, the severity table and these steps from what you learn.
