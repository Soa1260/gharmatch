# Verification and inspection guidelines

For the person on the team doing the checks. The three checks are different facts and are recorded separately at `/admin/verification`. Never record a check you did not do. A wrong "checked" is worse than none.

## General rules
- Look, record the type and the date, keep no copy. Do not photograph, download, forward or store documents or ID. No Aadhaar number is ever needed or recorded.
- Do the check in person or on a live video call, never by asking for documents over WhatsApp or email. That moves sensitive documents onto unsafe channels.
- If something looks wrong (mismatched names, a document that seems altered, an owner who will not meet), do not record anything. Note it in the audit log reason when you clear, or raise it as a report.
- Every change is written to the audit log with your account.

## 1. Identity Checked (the person)
- Ask to see a government photo ID with the person present, on a video call or in person. Check the photo matches and the name matches the account.
- Record the method (seen in person, or seen on video). The indicator says nothing about the property.
- Clear it if the person is found to have misrepresented themselves.

## 2. Ownership Documents Reviewed (the home)
- Acceptable: a recent municipal property-tax receipt, a utility bill for the property, a property card or relevant government record, or for someone acting for the owner a written authorization letter plus the owner's own check.
- Match the property address and the name to the listing and the person. Record the document type.
- This is not a legal title check. It means a document linking the person to the property was seen. Say so if asked.

## 3. Property Physically Inspected
- Visit the home in person, preferably with a second person, in daylight, and tell someone where you are.
- Tick only items you saw and tested yourself on the day:
  main-door security, door locks and latches (try them), window security, balcony security, entrance security, CCTV in common areas (see where cameras point), common-area lighting (check at dusk if you can), visitor management (ask how it works), intercom (try it), emergency exits (walk them), fire safety (extinguishers, escape route), parking security, building access control.
- Do not tick an item you only heard about. It stays "declared".
- Check cameras: they must be in common areas only, never pointing into a private space. If a camera points at a private space, do not record the inspection. Raise a report for undisclosed surveillance.
- Record the date you visited. An inspection is a snapshot. Re-inspect after major changes or complaints.
- A listing gets no inspection line without a recorded visit.
