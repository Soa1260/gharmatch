# Cold start: first 20 to 30 listings

A rental board with no homes is dead on arrival. Tenants will not post a search into an empty room, so landlords come first.

## Rules
- Only list a home with the owner's clear consent, ideally in writing (a WhatsApp message is enough).
- The owner creates or confirms the listing under their own account, or you post it on their behalf and they confirm. Never copy a listing from another site without permission.
- Never invent listings, photos or testimonials.

## Where to find owners (in rough order of yield)
1. **Your own network.** Friends and family with a flat to let, or who know a landlord. Ask for five introductions each.
2. **Housing society WhatsApp groups and notice boards** in Bopal, Shela, South Bopal, Thaltej, Kudasan, Sargasan. Ask the committee before posting.
3. **Local brokers who are fed up with fees.** Offer a free listing page for their owner clients. Keep them as friends, not enemies.
4. **“To let” boards** on the ground in a few target neighbourhoods. Call the number, explain, ask permission.
5. **Student and bachelor-heavy areas** near colleges and offices, where rules conflicts are most painful.

## The pitch to a landlord (30 seconds)
“I am building a free site where you write your house rules once, and only tenants who fit can ask. No broker fee, no calls from people who will not suit. Your number is shown only if you accept someone. Can I list your flat?”

## Targets
- Week 1: 10 listings across at least 4 areas and both cities.
- Week 2: 25 listings, at least 5 women-only or women-friendly homes with security features.
- Keep a sheet: owner, area, consent date, listing link, last confirmed date.
